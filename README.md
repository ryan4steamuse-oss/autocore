# AutoCore

### The All-in-One Smart Car Management Platform

**AutoCore** is an all-in-one vehicle management platform designed to make car ownership simpler, smarter, and more transparent.

From routine maintenance and service history to vehicle valuation, predictive maintenance, DIY repairs, compatible parts, modifications, theft protection, and mechanic comparisons, AutoCore brings the entire car ownership experience into one centralized platform.

Instead of relying on scattered service receipts, spreadsheets, mechanic recommendations, automotive forums, and multiple apps, AutoCore creates a **digital profile for your vehicle** and continuously helps you understand, maintain, protect, and improve it.

> **Know your car. Maintain it smarter. Spend less. Drive with confidence.**

*Most of these advertised features will be made available later on the app's lifecycle*

## 🔎 Overview

Owning a vehicle involves much more than simply driving it.

Drivers need to keep track of:

* Oil changes
* Brake replacements
* Tyre rotations
* Registration
* Insurance
* Inspections
* Service intervals
* Repairs
* Recalls
* Fuel consumption
* Vehicle depreciation
* Modifications
* Replacement parts
* Mechanic quotes
* Service records
* Unexpected breakdowns

For many vehicle owners, this information is scattered across emails, paper receipts, mechanic invoices, spreadsheets, manufacturer websites, and personal memory.

**AutoCore aims to change that.**

AutoCore creates a centralized digital identity for every vehicle and gives owners a single place to understand everything related to their car.

By using the vehicle's **VIN (Vehicle Identification Number)**, AutoCore can build a detailed vehicle profile and connect relevant information, maintenance schedules, service records, ownership costs, recommendations, and insights to that specific vehicle.

# 🚨 The Problem

Traditional car ownership is fragmented.

A driver may use:

* One application for navigation
* Another for fuel tracking
* Paper invoices for service history
* A manufacturer's website for recalls
* Google searches for repair information
* Automotive forums for advice
* Separate websites to compare mechanics
* Online stores to find compatible parts
* A spreadsheet to track expenses
* A dealership for servicing
* Another application for their dashcam

There is no single system that understands the **entire lifecycle of a vehicle**.

This creates several problems.

### 💸 Unexpected Costs

Drivers often don't know when maintenance is due until something goes wrong.

A small maintenance issue can eventually become an expensive repair.

### 🔧 Lack of Maintenance Knowledge

Many owners don't know:

* What maintenance their car requires
* When specific components should be replaced
* Whether a mechanic's recommendation is reasonable
* What parts are compatible
* Whether a repair can be performed themselves

### 🧾 Poor Service History

Service records are frequently stored as:

* Paper receipts
* Emails
* PDFs
* Photos
* Invoices

This makes it difficult to build a complete history of the vehicle.

### 💰 Lack of Pricing Transparency

Drivers may receive a repair quote without knowing:

* Whether the price is reasonable
* What the labour should cost
* What parts are being used
* Whether cheaper alternatives exist
* Whether the repair is actually necessary

### 📉 Lack of Ownership Insights

Most vehicle owners don't have a clear understanding of their vehicle's total cost of ownership.

AutoCore aims to provide that visibility.

---

# 💡 Our Solution

AutoCore turns a vehicle into a **digital asset with an intelligent management system**.

After adding a vehicle using its VIN, AutoCore can organize information about that vehicle into a centralized dashboard.

The platform is designed around several core principles:

### 🧠 Intelligence

Use AI and vehicle data to provide useful recommendations rather than simply displaying information.

### 🔧 Maintenance

Help owners maintain their vehicles before problems become expensive.

### 💰 Transparency

Give drivers better visibility into repair costs, vehicle value, and ownership expenses.

### 🛡️ Protection

Help users protect their vehicles against theft, unexpected failures, and poor maintenance.

### 🏎️ Personalization

Understand each vehicle and provide recommendations specific to its make, model, year, mileage, configuration, and owner preferences.

### 👥 Community

Connect drivers and enthusiasts around their vehicles, repairs, modifications, and automotive interests.

# 🤖 AI Predictive Maintenance

One of AutoCore's core goals is to move vehicle maintenance from **reactive to predictive**.

Rather than waiting for a component to fail, AutoCore can analyze available vehicle information and identify potential maintenance requirements.

The system could consider:

* Vehicle age
* Mileage
* Maintenance history
* Previous repairs
* Driving patterns
* Manufacturer service intervals
* Reported symptoms
* Diagnostic codes
* Vehicle health data
* Component replacement history

The AI could then provide recommendations such as:

> **Brake inspection recommended**

> Your vehicle has reached a mileage range where brake components should be inspected.

Or:

> **Battery replacement may be approaching**

> Based on the vehicle's age and previous battery replacement, consider testing the battery before the next long trip.

The objective isn't simply to generate notifications.

It is to provide **useful context and actionable recommendations**.

* Recent service
* Reported symptoms

**User:**

> My car makes a clicking noise when I turn left.

**AutoCore AI:**

> Based on your vehicle configuration and the symptom you've described, possible causes include CV joint wear, suspension components, or steering-related components.
